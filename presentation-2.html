01 · The Problem

A problem I started thinking about

🌏

Nepalis living abroad

People leave Nepal for education, employment and other opportunities. Participating in Nepal's electoral process from abroad can involve practical difficulties.

My question

“What if technology could make access to electoral information and future participation easier, while keeping the official election process secure?”

🇳🇵YuwaVoice Nepal
02 · The Idea

My startup idea: YuwaVoice Nepal

A neutral online platform that could provide accessible candidate information, issue-by-issue comparisons, civic information, and—if legally approved and technically feasible—a secure pathway for participation from abroad.

📋

Information

Clear, structured information from official or reliable sources.

⚖️

Comparison

Show the same issues for candidates without telling citizens who is “best.”

🔐

Future Security

Explore identity verification, privacy, auditability and secure participation.
